Install Our App

Get Salem Dominion Ministries on your device for easy access

Why Install Our App?

  • Fast access to sermons, events, and updates
  • Works offline - access content even without internet
  • Push notifications for important announcements
  • Native app experience on your device
  • Quick access to donation and prayer requests

Chrome / Android

  • 1
    Open our website in Chrome browser on your Android device
  • 2
    Look for the install prompt - Chrome will show "Add to Home screen" banner
  • 3
    Tap "Install" or "Add" to install the app on your device
  • 4
    Find the app on your home screen with the Salem Dominion Ministries icon

Safari / iPhone/iPad

  • 1
    Open our website in Safari browser on your iPhone or iPad
  • 2
    Tap the Share button (square icon with arrow) at the bottom of Safari
  • 3
    Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  • 4
    Tap "Add" in the top-right corner to confirm installation
  • 5
    Find the app on your home screen

Microsoft Edge

  • 1
    Open our website in Microsoft Edge browser
  • 2
    Click the App icon (three dots) in the top-right corner
  • 3
    Select "Apps" then "Install this site as an app"
  • 4
    Click "Install" to add the app to your device
